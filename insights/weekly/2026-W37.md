against **5:31/km @ HR 145 in March**, and has been flat since April. Garmin's

## Three changes for W38

1. **Do the 1 km repeats.** Third miss makes it a pattern, not a slip. Start at
   4 × 1 km @ 5:15–5:20, not the plan's 6 × 1 km @ 5:00 — 147 days without a
   sub-5:00 run means the plan's target is a month away, not a Tuesday away.
2. **Put hockey in the plan as a hard day.** Load 124 and anaerobic TE 3.1 is
   not a rest day. Don't schedule intervals within 24 h either side of it.
3. **Long run to 15 km.** Keep the +2 km/week ladder. The 13.7 was correctly
   paced at HR 138; hold that discipline as it lengthens.
